RecipePro helps you discover meals using ingredients already in your kitchen. RecipePro searches TheMealDB online and includes a curated fallback collection. AI generation and camera recognition are planned integrations.

A few things to know

Add ingredients individually or separated by commas. Tap an ingredient chip to edit it. Choose a category and dietary preferences, then find recipes. Recipe details clearly list what you have and what you still need.

Saved recipes stay in this browser on this device. When you search, up to three ingredients are sent to TheMealDB to find recipes. Saved online recipes are cached in this browser. No photos are uploaded. Check ingredient labels, allergies, freshness, and safe cooking practices before cooking.

Need help? Clear your filters if no recipes appear, or try a different ingredient.
